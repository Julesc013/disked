Existing explicit local implementation/test/repair and dev synchronization grant
applies to generated ordinary images. This review supplies no owner acceptance,
physical-media/elevation, installation, signing or publication authority.

The pre-evaluation contract binds exact expected source metadata, an owned input,
profile/window/finite limits and a nonzero capture epoch. The implementation
reuses the observation-worker host, its process/job/handle and identity machinery;
it introduces a private NTFS frame and payload adapter, not another scheduler.
The parent validates strict shapes, request/source/process/domain bindings,
retained native seek/read/error/byte facts and exact pure NTFS reconstruction.
Automatic3.0 selection is checked against the retained common-layout probe.
Metadata, cancellation, replay and applicability remain distinct facts.

The common host may observe a validated publication before its process exits.
The NTFS adapter deliberately keeps that result pending until actual owned child
exit; a controlled hold-after-publication test proves this boundary. A retained
immutable snapshot is tested after session teardown. Frame replay after removing
the original source succeeds without reopening it; inert preparation also works
with supplied metadata and no longer present source. This is historical evidence,
not permission to read a replacement file.

Exact clean qualification passes106 cases/eight methods and all20 selected x64
native groups, eight standalone headers and242 tooling tests(two skipped).
All1027 registered/build inputs are present and unchanged. Selected tests include
the existing NTFS supplied-byte/capture/file and exFAT reader/file/worker corpora.
Actual children cover auto/explicit3.0/3.1, profile mismatch/unsupported versions,
fragmented geometry and nonzero windows. Independent byte/read-order oracles,
source preservation, actual process PID/creation/job membership, corrupt frames
and replies, cancellation/hang/late/exit/startup failure and zero-wait retirement
are checked. Raw partial/native-error/binding observations remain inspectable
without applicability; adapter replay/profile disagreement is rejected.

Normal/fault executable import/security observations, strict target/include/header
boundaries, source map, manifest/context and exact compiler/SDK/runtime identities
pass. Four complete launch samples retain requests, output frames, executable and
source hashes, independently measured process identities and the live-publication
refusal. No whole build tree or generated image is committed. The full native
product suite and x86/old/other hosts were not qualified by this campaign; earlier
evidence remains bound to its own source and scope. Existing ordinary-file suites
still retain the WinError1314 file-symlink fixture gap rather than claiming a pass.

Source files remain under existing owners; CMake isolates normal/fault targets.
The current source map covers302 files. The8MiB payload and common128MiB process
budget do not constitute an OS CPU deadline, an atomic filesystem snapshot,
authenticated custody, complete NTFS inspection or renewed source/effect authority.
The adapter has no public command/frontend dispatch. Available scope can proceed
to shared frontend admission and original attribute-list/metadata/namespace/content
work. W075/report-preparation and whole-branch main qualification remain open;
owner, licensing, original platforms/storage, production writers/recovery and
