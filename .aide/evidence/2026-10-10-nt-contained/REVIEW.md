not independent/owner acceptance, provider admission or full-unit qualification.

The private expected capability, input/reply binding, lifecycle and finite
resource contract preceded evaluation. Source review checked reuse of existing

observer/worker/attempt identities; bounded publication and digest/producer

the full-table test did not reject mixed tables. The final source checks each

This does not qualify arbitrary callback wrappers or third-party provider code.

explicit refused native/full/mixed test table. No volume API is dispatched
through either refused selection.

and 24 controller invocations / 18 actual child launches / 116 assertions.

checkpoint cancellation, crash/hang outcomes, reader-only retirement, immutable

the owned controller; kernel job closure then signals that same child handle.

warnings remain errors. Both clean candidates ran the full tooling suite (215
